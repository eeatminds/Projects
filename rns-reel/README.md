# RNS College of Nursing: 20 s admissions reel (Meta ads)

**Deliverable:** `out/rns-reel-9x16.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz stereo, 20.0 s, about −14 LUFS.
It is ready for Reels and Stories placements. `out/rns-reel-thumbnail.jpg` is a cover frame taken at 1.6 s.

Every picture comes from rnsnursingcollege.com: real photos of the college, its lab and its hospital. Nothing is AI-generated. The music is an original track generated by `src/music.py`, so there are no licensing issues.

1. **Devika Mohan:** get her written consent to use her name, photo and testimonial in paid ads. Confirm she is still an Advanced Practice Nurse in Ireland. The site's testimonial is undated.
2. **WhatsApp number:** the site lists 99640 06920 as a phone number. Confirm it is on WhatsApp (ideally WhatsApp Business), and use the same number for the ad's "Send WhatsApp message" button.
3. **Hostel / Kerala food day:** the site has no hostel or food photos, so scene 4 uses the Onam celebration photo. The two claims come from the brief; make sure both are accurate.
4. **Ward photo:** patients appear small and at a distance. Confirm the college's consent covers promotional use, or swap in your own ward-round footage.

## Swapping in real footage

The stills version is complete and can run as-is. To replace a slot with real footage, drop a clip into `footage/` and rebuild:

| File | Slot | Shooting notes |
|---|---|---|
| `footage/s1.mp4` | 0–3 s | Student talks to camera. Frame the face in the **lower half**, because the hook text sits on the top half. |
| `footage/s2.mp4` | 3–8 s | Skills lab about 2.5 s, then ward rounds in the parent hospital. The text cards stay on top. |
| `footage/s3.mp4` | 8–14 s | Alumna abroad, in Malayalam: where she studied and where she works. Her own audio is used and the music ducks underneath. A lower-third with her name replaces the portrait card. Keep the signed consent on file. |
| `footage/s4.mp4` | 14–18 s | Hostel room, then the weekly Kerala food day. |
| `footage/music.wav` (or .mp3/.m4a) | 0–20 s | Optional: replaces the generated music bed. |

Clips can be any orientation. They are filled to 9:16, trimmed from their first frame to the slot length, and padded if they are short.

## Build

Requires Python 3 with numpy, Node with Playwright and Chromium, and ffmpeg.

```bash
python3 build.py
```

It takes about 75 s. `src/reel.html` is the composition and can be opened in a browser. Call `seek(t)` from the console to scrub to any time. `src/render.mjs --stills <dir> --at 1,5,9` renders single frames for review.
