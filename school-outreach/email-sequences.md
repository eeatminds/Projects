# School Outreach: Emails, LinkedIn & WhatsApp Scripts

**Positioning line (use everywhere):** *"I help schools complete their admission goals with parents who find them on Google."*

**Proof:** RNS International School is the **first web result** on Google for **"top school in rr nagar"**. The prospect can check it themselves, which makes it stronger than any claim.

**Offer:** a free **2-minute "Admission Search Audit" video**. That is the only call-to-action in the early emails. Ask for a small "yes" (the free video) before asking for the big one (a call, then ₹40k/month).

---

## Before you send anything

1. **Get RNSIS's permission** to name them in emails (one WhatsApp to your contact there is enough). While you're at it, ask for:
   - one number: *"How many admission enquiries came from Google/website this season?"*
   - a 1–2 line quote from the Principal or Admissions Head.

   Even *"enquiries from Google doubled"* in their words will lift replies a lot. Put it into Email 2 when you have it.
2. **Don't pitch RNSIS's direct competitors.** Skip any school in RR Nagar, Kengeri, Nagarbhavi, Ullal, Vijayanagar or on Mysore Road. Two rows in `prospects.csv` are marked **HOLD** for this reason. Pitch the *same result in a different area* instead: "I did it for RR Nagar, I can do it for Whitefield."
3. **Verify before you send.** The `[VERIFY]` lines in Sequence B are based on tool estimates. Do a 30-second incognito Google search before sending and put in what you actually see.
4. **Fill `{{area}}`** from the `Area to pitch` column. If it says `[check Maps]`, look the school up on Google Maps. Wherever you see `{{area}}`, you can write "near me" instead.

## LinkedIn: use it the same day as Email 1

LinkedIn is where the **image works best**, because there's no spam filter. It needs no email setup, so **you can start today**.

**Connection note (under 200 characters):**
```
Hi {{first_name}}, I help Bangalore schools get found by parents on Google
(we got RNS International to #1 for "top school in rr nagar"). Would love to
connect.
```

**DM after they accept (attach the image):**
```
Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}!

Here's the RNS International School result I mentioned [attach image].
Parents near {{school}} search the same way ("best school in {{area}}").

If useful, I can record a free 2-minute video showing where {{school}}
shows up today. Want me to?
```

Limits: about **15–20 connection requests per day**. LinkedIn caps free accounts at roughly 100 per week. On free accounts, personalised notes are also limited, so save notes for HOT and WARM prospects.

## How to reply when they answer

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"Yes, send the video"** | "Great, you'll have it by [tomorrow]. Who else on your team should see it?" (This finds the real decision maker.) |
| **"Send me details / a brochure"** | "Happy to. Rather than a generic PDF, let me send a 2-minute video specific to [School]. Is this email the best place?" |
| **"What does it cost?"** | "Plans start at ₹40,000/month (about $500). For most schools, one admission's annual fee covers the first three months. I'd rather show you the audit first so you can judge whether it's worth it." |
| **"We already have an agency"** | "Good to hear. Are they getting you onto page one for '[area] school' searches? If yes, you're in good hands. If not, I'm happy to do a free second-opinion audit. No obligation." |
| **"Admissions are full / not now"** | "Congratulations! Honestly, that's the best time to start. Rankings take 3–4 months to build, so starting now means [School] is on top *before* the 2028-29 enquiry season opens in October." |
| **"SEO takes too long"** | "The map results (Google Business Profile) can move within weeks, and that's where 'school near me' clicks go. The bigger rankings take 3–4 months, but once you're there, you stay without paying per click." |
| **"Can you guarantee #1?"** | "No one honest can, and Google itself warns against anyone who does. What I can show you is that I've done it for RNS, and I'll report rankings and enquiries every month." |
| **"No"** | "Understood, thanks for letting me know. All the best for admissions." (Then stop. Mark them *Lost* in the sheet.) |

## The 2-minute audit video (your conversion engine)

Record with **Loom** (free plan) or your phone's screen recorder. Keep it under 2.5 minutes. Say their name and their school in the first 5 seconds.

1. **(0:00)** "Hi [Name], this is Shreyas. Here's what parents see when they search for a school near [School]."
2. **(0:15)** Open an incognito Google search for "best school in [area]". Show where they appear and who's above them.
3. **(0:45)** Show the map results. Compare their **star rating and review count** with the top competitor.
4. **(1:15)** Open their website on mobile. Is there a clear "Admissions 2027-28" button? Is there a WhatsApp button? Is it fast?
5. **(1:40)** "Three things I'd fix first: [1], [2], [3]. This is what I did for RNS International School, now #1 for 'top school in rr nagar'."
6. **(2:00)** "If you'd like, I can walk you through the plan in 15 minutes. Just reply with a time."

## The 15-minute call

1. **Their goal (5 min).** "How many seats are you aiming to fill for 2027-28? How are you doing against that?" "Where do most enquiries come from today?" "What are you spending on ads?"
2. **Gap (3 min).** Share the audit findings and point to the 1–2 searches they're losing.
3. **Proof (2 min).** The RNSIS screenshot (and RNSIT's 3× enquiries from Google Ads, from your website).
4. **Offer (3 min).** The *Admissions on Google: 90-day plan* (below).
5. **Close (2 min).** "If I can start on [date], you'd see the map listing improve before admissions close. Shall I send the proposal today?"

**₹40,000/month (about $500), 3-month minimum.** Gulf schools: **$500–650/month**.

- A monthly report: rankings + enquiries from Google
- *Optional add-on:* Google Ads management (RNSIT case: 3× qualified enquiries)
