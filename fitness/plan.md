# V-Taper Plan

Start: Wed 11 Nov 2026 · Baseline body scan: 7 Oct 2026

A V-taper = **wide back + wide shoulders + small waist**. For you the fastest route is:
**lose belly fat first** (diet + steps), while **lifting heavy for back and shoulders** so the muscle stays and grows.

## 2. Goal and timeline

**Phase 1: fat loss (Nov 2026 → end of Mar 2027, ~20 weeks)**
Target: ~68 kg, ~12–13% body fat, waist down ~8–10 cm, 10 strict pull-ups. Belly flat, V-shape visible.

**Phase 2: lean bulk (Apr 2027 → ~Nov 2027)**
Eat slightly above maintenance (+0.25 kg/week) to make shoulders and back bigger while staying lean.
This is what turns "lean" into a strong V-taper.

## 3. Daily targets (Phase 1)

| Target | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | **2,000 kcal** |
| Protein | **140 g** (never below 130 g) |
| Fat | ~55 g |
| Carbs | the rest (~220 g) |
| Water | 3 L+ |
| Steps | 10,000 |
| Sleep | 7–8 h |

How it was set: maintenance is about 2,500–2,600 kcal (body ~1,700 + office job + gym + steps).
2,000 kcal = ~500–600 kcal deficit = ~0.5 kg fat loss per week. Protein at ~1.8 g/kg protects muscle.

**Weekly adjustment rules** (based on the weekly average weight):

- Losing 0.4–0.7 kg/week → keep going, change nothing.
- Losing less than 0.3 kg for 2 weeks in a row → first add 2,000 steps/day; if still stuck, cut 150 kcal.
- Losing more than 1 kg/week (after the first 2 weeks) → add 150 kcal.
- Always tired / strength dropping a lot → tell me, we take a 1-week diet break at maintenance.

Good breakfast. Optional upgrades:

- More fruit: guava, apple, papaya, pomegranate or orange are best (filling, low calorie). Have them as the evening snack instead of adding to breakfast.

### Whey protein: 2 scoops a day

Timing barely matters — **total protein per day** is what builds muscle. Use whey where your meals are low in protein:

- **Scoop 1:** mid-morning at office (~11 am), in water. Or stir into the oats after cooking (don't boil whey).
- **Scoop 2:** within 1–2 h after your workout. On rest days, have it as a 5 pm snack.

### Lunch (office: rice + sambar)

This is your weakest meal (lots of rice, little protein). Fix it like this:

1. **Rice: 1 cup cooked** (about one katori). Ask for less rice, more sambar.
2. **Sambar: 2 katoris.**
3. Take any vegetable side (poriyal / kootu / salad) and curd or buttermilk if available.
4. **Carry one protein from home**, e.g.:
   - 3 boiled eggs (216 kcal, 19 g protein)
   - 100 g paneer bhurji (~280 kcal, 18 g)

   - 100 g chicken (~165 kcal, 31 g cooked weight)
5. Skip papad, fried sides and sweets (save them for the free meal).

### Simple food rules

- Protein in every meal. Eat the protein first.
- Tea/coffee: max 2 cups, max 1 tsp sugar each.

- Alcohol slows fat loss a lot. Keep it rare.

## 5. Daily non-negotiables: "50 / 10 / 10k"

Same every day, gym day or not. ~15 minutes total, spread through the day.

| # | Habit | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **50 push-ups** | 5 sets of 10, any time of day |
| 2 | **10 pull-ups** | As singles spread through the day (1 rep each time you pass the bar). Never to failure. |
| 3 | **10,000 steps** | Walk after lunch and after dinner (10–15 min each helps a lot) |
| 4 | **Stomach vacuum** | 3 × 20 s every morning, empty stomach. Breathe out fully, pull belly button to spine, hold. Trains the deep core that keeps the waist tight. |
| 5 | **Hit 140 g protein / ~2,000 kcal** | Send me every meal |
| 6 | **3 L water, 7–8 h sleep** | Poor sleep = more hunger + less fat loss |

Buy a doorway pull-up bar for home (₹800–1,500). It's the single best tool for a V-taper.

**How the numbers grow:**
- Push-ups: +10 every 4 weeks → 100 a day by end of March.
- Pull-ups: 10 singles → when easy, 5 × 2 → 4 × 3 → 3 × 4 … Retest your max every 4 weeks.

## 6. Gym: 4 days a week, ~60 min

Schedule: **Mon Upper A · Tue Lower A · Thu Upper B · Fri Lower B**. Wed, Sat, Sun: rest (still do 50/10/10k).

Warm up 5 min (light cardio + 1–2 light sets of the first exercise).
Leave 1–2 reps in the tank on each set. Rest 2–3 min on big lifts, 60–90 s on small ones.

### Upper A (back width)
| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Wide-grip lat pulldown | 4 × 8–12 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 3 × 8–12 |
| Seated cable row | 3 × 10–12 |
| Dumbbell lateral raise | 4 × 12–20 |
| Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3 × 8–12 |
| Face pull | 3 × 15 |
| Biceps curl + triceps pushdown (superset) | 2 × 12 each |

### Lower A
| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Squat (or leg press) | 3 × 6–10 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 |
| Walking lunges | 2 × 10 per leg |
| Leg curl | 2 × 12 |
| Calf raise | 3 × 12–15 |
| Hanging knee raise | 3 × 12 |

### Upper B (back thickness + shoulders)
| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Pull-ups (assisted machine/band if needed) | 4 × 5–8 |
| Flat dumbbell or barbell bench press | 3 × 6–10 |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 10 per side |
| Cable or machine lateral raise | 4 × 12–20 |
| Cable fly / pec deck | 2 × 12–15 |
| Rear-delt fly | 3 × 15 |
| Hammer curl + overhead triceps extension (superset) | 2 × 12 each |

### Lower B
| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Trap-bar or regular deadlift | 3 × 5 |
| Leg press | 3 × 10–12 |
| Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 10 per leg |
| Leg extension | 2 × 12–15 |
| Calf raise | 3 × 15 |
| Plank | 3 × 45–60 s |

Why so much back and side-shoulder work: lats and side delts are what make the "V". Lateral raises are done 8 sets a week on purpose.
Why legs: they burn the most calories and keep the body balanced. Don't skip them.

**How to progress (double progression):** pick a weight. When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, go up in weight next time (2.5 kg for big lifts, 1–2 kg for dumbbells). Write your weights down, or tell me and I'll log them.

**Optional cardio:** 20 min incline treadmill walk after Lower days. This is the first thing we add if fat loss stalls (before cutting food).

**No gym?** Home version: replace pulldowns with pull-ups/negatives, presses with push-up variations, lateral raises with dumbbells or bands, legs with goblet squats, lunges, single-leg RDLs.
